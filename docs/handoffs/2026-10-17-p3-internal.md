## Real-data validation correction

The initial baseline failed before saving epoch 1: three boxes on a 400x312 image
were clipped to y1=y2=312. A diagnostic replay confirmed finite boxes/scores and
valid labels. Installed TorchVision SSD source clips boxes before NMS without
removing zero-area boxes. Prediction checks now count/exclude zero-area boxes,
while rejecting inverted, out-of-bounds or non-finite outputs. Each epoch logs
valid/discarded counts. A regression fixture covers both clipped and inverted
boxes. Failed runs remain separate; no failed run is called a trained baseline.
